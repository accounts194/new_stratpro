Who We Are

Stratpro HVACR & Building Solutions Pvt Ltd is a premier engineering firm based in Bengaluru. We operate as an authorized channel partner for Carrier Midea India and Toshiba Carrier, bringing world-class thermal management and air quality solutions to residential, commercial, and industrial clients.

We believe that modern comfort should not come at the cost of the environment. Our engineering approach prioritizes lifecycle efficiency, reducing energy draw while maximizing equipment lifespan.

The Stratosphere Story

Our name comes from the stratosphere — home to the ozone layer that shields the Earth from harmful UV radiation. It's our promise: every system we design aims to protect, not strain, the environment it serves.

This philosophy guides everything we do — from working with industry-approved refrigerants to designing intelligent controls that eliminate waste.

Just as the stratosphere quietly protects everything below it, we design systems that work hard in the background — so your space stays comfortable without costing the planet.
